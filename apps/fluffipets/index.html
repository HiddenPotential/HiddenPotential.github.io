Fluffipets

A small animal who lives on your Mac desktop.

He walks along the edges of your screen, follows your cursor when you move it, dozes in a corner when you leave him alone, and climbs across your open windows as if they were ledges. He is not a game you play. He is company while you work.

He never gets in your way

  • Never takes keyboard focus — not when he speaks, not when he moves
  • Never eats a click; everything underneath him stays clickable
  • Silent unless you turn sound on
  • Steps away from the area you are typing in
  • No Dock icon, no Command-Tab entry

He never dies

No decay, no guilt, no punishment for a weekend away. The attachment here is built out of company, not anxiety.

Completely offline

No account, no sign-in, no network, no analytics, no ads. Nothing about you leaves your Mac. Read the privacy policy.

Fluffipets Pro

The pet is free and complete. One purchase adds reminders delivered by the pet himself, the growth journal, and every character skin. No subscription.

Requirements

macOS 13 Ventura or later.
