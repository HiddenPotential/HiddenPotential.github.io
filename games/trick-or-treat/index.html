Trick or Treat Street 🎃

It's Halloween night! Pick a costume (each one has its own power), then walk up to houses and knock for candy. Most doors give you a treat, but some play a trick on you, and ghosts are floating down the street. Get home by midnight with the biggest haul you can! Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, drag to look around, and turn your sound on for spooky music. 🎧 Click the game first so it hears your keys. It's even better full screen.
